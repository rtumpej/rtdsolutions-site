GEO vs SEO: what changes when buyers ask AI

SEO is about appearing high in Google's list of results. GEO (generative engine optimisation) is about being named in the answer of an AI assistant, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Copilot. An assistant shows no list. It writes one answer and names a few companies in it. Whether you are one of them is a new question.

The difference on one page

SEO (Google)GEO (AI assistants)
What the buyer sees10 links and adsOne answer with a few names and sources
What counts as successYour rank, a click to your siteThe assistant names you or cites your page
What decides itYour site, links to it, technical healthMostly what other sites say about you: directories, sector and tourism sites, press, reviews, comparison sites. Your own site comes second.
LanguageUsually one language, one countryEach language gets a different answer. A German buyer is given other names than a Dutch one.
How stableRanks move slowlyOne answer is a draw: the same question gives a different list almost every time. What is stable is the share of answers that name you, measured over enough answers.
How to measureGoogle Search ConsoleAsk the same questions several times, to several assistants, in several languages, and report the share of answers with a range

Three facts

  1. Where AI answers, people click less

    Google users clicked a result on 8% of visits when an AI summary was shown, against 15% without one. Pew Research, July 2025

  2. AI relies on third-party sources more than on your own site

    A University of Toronto study found that AI search engines strongly prefer independent, third-party sources over brand-owned pages. Chen et al., 2025

    Our own test, 14-15 Oct 2026: 150 ChatGPT and Perplexity answers about places to stay in Slovenia, in five languages. The most cited source was the national tourism site, slovenia.info (169 of 1192 sources, 14%). The index

  3. Same question, different answer

    ChatGPT and Google's AI return the same list of recommended brands less than 1 time in 100. SparkToro, Jan 2026

    Our test: when we asked the same questions about places to stay a second time, only 35% of mentions came back (same place, same language, same assistant). In three identical runs, only 12% of places were named every time. But the share of answers is stable: when we asked every question again the next day, none of 127 places changed its share for real. So we measure the share of answers, not single answers.

What it means for you

  • SEO stays the foundation. Assistants still search the web, and a page they cannot find does not exist for them.
  • GEO adds three things: clear answers to your buyers' questions on your site, in your buyers' languages; correct facts where AI reads (directories, chambers, tourism sites, comparison and booking profiles); and regular measurement of your share of answers, with the same questions.
  • Nobody can guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend you. Anyone who promises that is not telling the truth. What you can do is measure where you stand and fix the sources the assistants rely on.
