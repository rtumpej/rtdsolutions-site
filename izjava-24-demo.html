      Source of the fixed text: Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, Annex V Part A (OJ L 165, 29.6.2023, p. 1), the official version in each language, retrieved from the EU Publications Office on 14 Oct 2026. Point 6 cites the acts with their titles and Official Journal references (Art. 21(3)). Directive 2014/30/EU has no Irish version, so the Irish page cites it by number. Points 3 (lifting machinery assembled on site) and 8 (notified body) do not apply to this machine and show "—"; point 9 (Module A, internal production control) applies. The declaration may be given digitally if the instructions say where to find it (Art. 10(8)); it must then stay online until the end of the machine's expected lifetime and for at least 10 years after it is placed on the market or put into service. The page controls (print, contact, dates) use symbols and ISO dates, so they need no translation. The standards list is illustrative.
