Who does AI recommend? Places to stay in Slovenia

More and more guests skip Google and ask ChatGPT or another AI assistant: "Where is a good family glamping site?" The assistant shows no list of links. It writes one answer and names a few places in it. Who is named depends on the guest's language, the assistant and the moment. So we ask every question several times and count the share of answers that name a place.

Key findings

  1. Each language gets its own list. For farm stays, ChatGPT named 43 farms over three runs in five languages; 28 of them in one language only, none in all five. A German guest is given other farms than an Italian one.
  2. The assistants rarely agree. For the same question in the same language, the two assistants named few of the same places: the mean overlap was 10%.
  3. One answer is a draw; the share is stable. When we asked the same questions a second time, only 35% of mentions came back (same place, same language, same assistant). In three identical English runs on ChatGPT, only 5 of 43 places (12%) were named every time. But the share of answers held: when we asked every question again the next day, none of 127 places changed its share for real. So the index reports shares with a range, not single answers.
  4. Big names are stable, small ones are not. For a wellness weekend, the assistants named Terme Olimia (Hotel Sotelia) in 27 of 30 answers (range 82-95%). Of 44 farm stays named, 20 appeared in just one answer out of 30.
  5. In the Soča valley the slot is empty. One of the assistants named a place to stay in only 3 of 15 answers, each time in Italian. Otherwise it said "stay in Bovec or Kobarid". Anyone can take that slot.
  6. The two assistants read different sources. ChatGPT often relied on providers' own sites (42% of its sources), Perplexity on booking platforms (45%).

What a place to stay can do

  • Check your listing on slovenia.info and on your local tourism board's site. Is it complete, and does it exist in your guests' languages? Slovenia.info was the most cited source (169 of 1192 sources, in 66 of 150 answers).
  • Answer guests' questions on your own site in their language: who the place suits, what is nearby, prices and season.
  • Keep your booking platform profiles in order. One of the assistants relied on them for almost half of its sources (45%).
  • Measure regularly, with the same questions. One answer is not a result. What counts is the share of answers that name you, and whether it moves more than chance would move it.

Method

  • Questions. Five guest questions: family glamping; a quiet boutique hotel near Bled or Bohinj; a farm stay with children; where to stay in the Soča valley for rafting and hiking; a wellness weekend for two.
  • Languages. Each question in five languages: Slovenian, English, German, Italian and Dutch. The user location was the language's country (Slovenia, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands); English had no location.
  • Assistants. ChatGPT through OpenAI's official API (gpt-5-mini with web search) and Perplexity through its official API (preset "fast").
  • Runs. Each question was asked three times of each assistant: twice on 14 Oct 2026 between 14:40 and 16:00 CEST and once on 15 Oct 2026 at 01:15 CEST. On 14 Oct the five English questions were asked of ChatGPT three more times to measure how single answers change.
  • Size. 150 answers and 1192 cited sources, plus 15 answers for the change test.
  • Share and range. For each place we count in how many of the 30 answers to one guest question it is named. The range is a 90% interval (Wilson) that allows for how much answers to the same question differ between runs. Below 10 answers we give the count only, never a percentage.
  • Pooling the two days. We compared the 15 Oct answers with those of 14 Oct: none of 127 places changed its share for real (correlation of the shares 0.83). So we count the three runs as one measurement.
  • Real change. Between two editions we call a change real only if a shuffle test of the answers within each question gives p < 0.05 and the change is at least 5 percentage points. With 30 answers per place this means about 20 points or more in practice. Smaller moves are reported as "no measurable change". On two runs where nothing had changed, the rule raised no false alarm (0 of 113 places); a simple "moved 10 points" rule would have raised 87.
  • Matching names. We matched place names with a hand-made list (with spelling variants), checked against the answers of all three runs. A place counts as named if its name appears in the answer text.
  • Sources. Every cited source was classed as the provider's own site, a tourism board, a booking or comparison site, or other.

Limits

  • Two days of measurement, two assistants (ChatGPT and Perplexity). We did not measure Claude, Gemini or Copilot.
  • The range describes how answers vary between runs a few hours or days apart. When a provider changes its model or search index, answers can change more. That is a real change in the answers, not a measurement error.
  • Answers through the official APIs can differ from what a person sees in the app (memory, settings, exact location, model version).
  • Names were matched with a list. A place the assistant spelled very differently may have been missed.
  • We measure what the assistants answer. We do not measure what is truly best, and we do not rate providers.
  • Nobody can guarantee that an AI will recommend a given provider.

Listed here? Correction or removal

The index reports what the assistants answered. It is not a rating and not a ranking. If a name is spelled or attributed wrongly, or you want it removed:

  1. Email info@rtdsolutions.si with the subject "Index: correction" or "Index: removal". Give the name of the place and its website.
  2. We reply within three working days and fix an error as soon as we have checked it.
  3. On request we remove a name from the published tables within five working days, no reason needed. The totals stay the same. If the name contains a person's name (a farm, for example), we remove it at once.
  4. Every change is logged below, with its date.
