Questions

Is this the same as SEO?

Partly. In SEO you compete for a place in a list of results. An AI assistant writes one answer and names a few companies in it. Whether you are among them depends on what other sites say about you, how clearly your own site is written, and whether the assistant can read it at all. This is also called GEO, generative engine optimisation.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend us?

No. Nobody can. Answers change from question to question and from week to week. We measure where you stand, show what to fix, and measure every month whether assistants name you more often.

Do you write fake reviews or hidden ads?

No. No fake reviews, no undisclosed advertorials, no fake profiles. The fixes are your own content, listings in real directories and clear facts on your own site.

Which languages?

Any EU language. We ask the assistants in the language your buyers use and write the fix pages in that language.

Who does the work?

Measurements and drafts are prepared with AI tools. A person reviews every full report before it is sent.
